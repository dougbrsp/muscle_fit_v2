Premium anatomical muscle map illustration for a dark-themed fitness app.

COMPOSITION: two full-body human figures side by side on a wide canvas. LEFT figure = anterior view (front of the body facing the viewer). RIGHT figure = posterior view (back of the body facing the viewer). Both figures have exactly the same height (about 88% of the canvas height), the same scale and the same pose, centered vertically, with a clear empty gap between the two figures. Orthographic, perfectly frontal, perfectly symmetrical, no perspective, no rotation. Neutral anatomical standing pose: arms relaxed and held slightly away from the torso (about 20 degrees) so the arms never overlap the torso, palms facing forward, small simple hands with fingers together, legs straight and slightly apart so each inner thigh is visible on its own.

ANATOMY: écorché style (skin removed), athletic, gender-neutral body with correct proportions. Every major muscle group is a clearly separated shape outlined by a crisp dark groove, so each group can be identified at a glance:
- front view: pectoralis major, anterior and lateral deltoids, biceps, forearm muscles, rectus abdominis with six-pack segments, external obliques on the sides of the waist, serratus anterior, quadriceps (rectus femoris, vastus lateralis, vastus medialis), adductors on the inner thigh, tibialis anterior and the calves seen from the front, neck muscles and upper trapezius.
- back view: trapezius (upper and middle, diamond shape), rear deltoids, infraspinatus and teres, latissimus dorsi (wide wings), erector spinae of the lower back, triceps, forearm extensors, gluteus maximus and medius, hamstrings, adductors seen from behind, gastrocnemius and soleus.

STYLE AND COLOR: monochrome graphite only, like a polished dark-metal anatomical statue. Muscles in medium-dark graphite greys between #3A3A3A and #5C5C5C with soft satin highlights up to #8C8C8C and subtle muscle-fiber striations; grooves between muscles near #1A1A1A. Soft studio light from above and a thin neutral rim light outlining the silhouette so it reads well on a near-black background. No other colors at all: no orange, no red, no blue, no skin tones. Head is a smooth featureless mannequin head (no face, no hair). 

BACKGROUND: fully transparent. No floor, no cast shadow, no backdrop, no frame, no panel, no text, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no arrows, no lines pointing to muscles, no logos, no watermark.
